A user story map gives a team a shared view of the product journey. Activities sit across the top, tasks sit beneath them, and user stories are arranged into release slices from the smallest valuable version to future work.

## Start with the user journey

Choose one persona and describe what they are trying to accomplish. Add broad activities from left to right, such as **Discover**, **Choose**, **Pay**, and **Manage order**. Keep the map focused on one journey rather than the entire product.

## Add tasks and stories

Tasks describe what the user does. Stories describe the smallest valuable capability that helps them do it. Keep stories specific enough to discuss with product, design, QA, and engineering.

## Slice the releases

Place the smallest coherent journey in **MVP**, the next valuable improvements in **Release 2**, and unresolved or lower-priority work in **Later**. A release slice should tell a usable story, not just collect technically related tasks.
