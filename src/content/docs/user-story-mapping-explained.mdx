## Activities

Activities are the large steps in a user journey. They form the backbone of the map and are usually arranged from left to right.

## Tasks

Tasks are the meaningful things a user does within an activity. They help the team discuss the journey without jumping immediately into implementation details.

## User stories

User stories are smaller slices of value that can be discussed, designed, built, and tested. Several stories can sit under one task.

## Release slices

Release rows help the team decide what belongs in the MVP, what comes next, and what can wait. The goal is to make trade-offs visible and keep a release focused on user value.
